Our Approach

Co-Developed Methodology

A people-driven framework for collecting, mapping, and integrating community feedback into location-specific action.

How It All Comes Together

From community input to decisions on the ground — four steps that close the loop between people and place.

01

Capture

Residents share location-specific feedback directly in place, anchored to where it actually happens. Our digital tools make it easy for anyone to contribute — on any device, in any language.

02

Map

Contributions come together on the map, turning community knowledge into visible spatial insights. Patterns, clusters, and gaps emerge — revealing the human experience of place.

03

Prioritise

Insights are ranked by frequency and relevance, so the patterns that matter most rise to the top. AI-powered analytics surfaces themes, sentiments, and urgent needs — without burying important signals.

04

Act

Findings feed straight into better design, planning, and decisions on the ground. Evidence-based case files equip planners and architects to connect real community signals with the spatial levers that drive impact.

Built for the Cities of Tomorrow

City Layers combines participatory methodology, real-time mapping, and AI-powered analytics to make urban participation meaningful, accessible, and impossible to ignore.

01

Participatory Mapping

Residents pin feelings, observations, and ideas directly onto the city. Every contribution is geolocated, structured, and anchored to the exact place it describes — creating a ground-truth layer of lived experience.

02

Place Intelligence

Our methodology clusters community signals, surfaces spatial patterns, and reveals the urban dynamics that lie beneath the numbers. The result is evidence-based place intelligence that planners can actually use.

03

Natural Language Querying

Ask questions about your data in plain language and get instant answers. Simply type "What are the most requested improvements in the eastern district?" and get a clear, actionable response — no SQL required.

04

Inclusive Engagement Tools

A full suite of participation tools — surveys, idea boards, guided worksheets, and mapping walks — designed to be accessible to all community members regardless of age, language, or digital literacy.

05

Feedback-to-Action Workflows

City Layers closes the loop between mapping and decision-making. Structured reports, heat maps, and stakeholder-ready summaries translate community input into the evidence that moves projects forward.

06

Accessibility by Design

From multilingual support to offline-capable worksheets, our tools are built for the full diversity of a community. Participation should not require a smartphone, a broadband connection, or a planning degree.
