Our Approach

A Community-Driven Methodology

City Layers was developed from the ground up to activate participation, map local knowledge, and reveal how people experience places.

Involving communities in design and planning
brings better outcomes.

City Layers uses an integrated methodology for activating participation, sharing local knowledge, and understanding community experience in place. People contribute observations, needs, barriers, ideas, and experiences directly on a digital map. City Layers then structures this input into spatial insights that show where issues occur, what patterns emerge, and what should be prioritised. This creates a clear link between public participation and location-specific decisions.

How It All Comes Together

Four steps to close the loop between people and decisions.

01

ASK

Every campaign starts by defining what to listen for, accessibility, safety, mobility, climate comfort, or whatever matters most, with participants sharing feedback directly where it happens, no app to download.

02

MAP

Observations are placed on a shared map and structured into context. You can explore that structure through heatmaps, clusters, graphs, and other analysis views, the building blocks of City Layers' spatial intelligence layer.

03

ANALYSE

City Layers transforms data into spatial patterns and insights. Where available, that ranking is cross-checked against objective datasets, so priorities reflect both what people report and what's measurable on the ground.

04

PRIORITISE

Prioritised insights become structured evidence reports, ready for planning decisions or direct stakeholder communication. This is where community input stops being feedback and starts improving design, planning, and decision-making on the ground.

Turning Input into Intelligence

Providing advanced analytics for place intelligence.

When communities are engaged, it's important to document, structure, and integrate their knowledge and needs into decisions. This is where we make a difference. City Layers turns location-specific community knowledge into themes, hot spots, recurring patterns, action priorities and reports.

Every insight remains traceable to the real mapped contributions behind it. Our methodology helps reveal patterns across community input and provides spatial insights ready to act upon. Decisions about what to prioritise, where to act, and how to respond stay with the experts and decision-makers using City Layers.

This is how community mapping becomes actionable place intelligence: spatial insight that helps teams understand how people experience places and move from feedback to action.
