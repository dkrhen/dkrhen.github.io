From city detectives to city planners: The city through the eyes of children

The Urban Observations project demonstrates a systematic methodology for translating the ground-level urban experiences of children into actionable, structured data to assist urban planners and municipal decision-makers. Utilizing the City Layers citizen science platform and a specialized physical-digital toolkit, the project successfully mapped over 7,000 data points and 100 design proposals from children aged 8–18 across Vienna, Austria.
