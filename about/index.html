Our Purpose

Technology is our medium, People are our purpose

We believe that everyone should have a voice in shaping the cities where they live, work, and play.

Our Story

City Layers was born from a simple observation: urban planning processes often exclude the very people they're meant to serve. Traditional public consultations are time-consuming, inaccessible, and fail to capture the diversity of community perspectives.

We set out to change that. By combining innovative technology with inclusive design principles, we've created a platform that makes urban participation accessible, engaging, and impactful for everyone.

Today, City Layers empowers communities and planners across Europe to collaborate on creating better, more livable urban spaces. From neighborhood improvements to city-wide strategic planning, we're helping to build the participatory cities of tomorrow.
