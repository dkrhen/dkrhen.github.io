Participatory Mapping

A General term used to define a set of approaches and techniques that combine the tools of modern cartography with participatory methods to record and represent the spatial knowledge of local communities.

Urban Commons

Urban commons are shared city resources—such as community gardens, public squares, and co-working spaces—that are collectively managed and governed by local communities rather than solely by private markets or the government.
