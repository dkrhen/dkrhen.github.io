Use Cases

Participation isn't only about streets and parks.

City Layers works wherever people's experience of a place needs to be understood, measured, and acted on. Here are some of the contexts we work in.

Sustainable Urban Mobility Plans For urban planners
Mobility planning image

Under the revised EU TEN-T regulation, 431 European cities must adopt a Sustainable Urban Mobility Plan by 2027 and keep it monitored over time. Community engagement isn't optional. Every plan has to be built in partnership with the people who live there.

City Layers gives you the tools and the methodology to run that engagement properly, and to walk away with structured spatial data you can actually use. Not another consultation report that gathers dust, but evidence that feeds directly into your plan.

What we can offer:
  • For urban planning

    Place Audit as a low-barrier entry point.

  • For cities

    Experience Monitoring as a post-SUMP evaluation tool.

Participatory Mapping for Public Space & Street Redesign For community development
Public space redesign image

Urban design and planning projects always require public participation, but practitioners lack the tools to understand community insights at a spatial level. City Layers bridges that gap — turning resident experience into location-specific evidence that architects and planners can build from.

What we can offer:
  • For urban planning

    Community mapping campaigns.

  • For community development

    A tool to collect, map, and integrate location-specific feedback into decisions.

  • For researchers

    Collect and structure spatial observations for analysis and reporting.

Passenger Experience for Transport Stations & Operations For transport operators
Transport station image

Transport operators invest significantly in improving station environments, but struggle with collecting and integrating passenger feedback into decisions. As a result, operators can't clearly identify where issues occur and what to fix first. City Layers maps that experience to the station floor.

What we can offer:
  • For cities

    A spatial reporting system that shows where passenger experience breaks down and how to intervene.

  • For transport operators

    Continuous insight into where the passenger journey underperforms, supporting targeted investment decisions.

Customer Experience for Shopping Centres & Retail Assets For retail
Shopping centre image

Shopping centres want to maximise dwell time and revenue, but existing customer experience data lacks insight into how customers actually experience specific locations within the asset. City Layers surfaces that spatial intelligence — pinpointing where the experience is working and where it is losing people.

What we can offer:
  • For shopping malls

    Continuous insight into where visitor experience breaks down to support targeted improvements.

  • For retail asset managers

    A spatial layer of customer sentiment to sit alongside footfall and sales data.

Mobility Infrastructure Planning For transport authorities
Active mobility image

Cycling and walking infrastructure is only effective if it matches how people actually move and where they feel unsafe. Traditional traffic counts and desire-line analysis miss the lived dimension — the junction that feels dangerous, the missing shelter, the route nobody takes after dark. City Layers captures that ground-level knowledge at scale.

What we can offer:
  • For cities and municipalities

    Map where residents feel unsafe cycling or walking to prioritise infrastructure investment.

  • For urban planners

    Identify desire lines and missing connections through community-generated spatial data.

  • For transport authorities

    Validate cycling network proposals against lived experience before committing to construction.
