Platform Access

A map-based community engagement platform for organisations running their own projects and campaigns. You run it yourself.

Create and publish your own map-based campaigns, invite people to contribute on site or remotely, and watch the results come in. Responses become patterns, dashboards, and reports automatically; everything in one platform. Your team runs it independently, with onboarding and support from us.

  • A dedicated workspace for running and managing map-based campaigns
  • Campaign creation adapted to different needs - built from scratch, step by step or with a conversational assistant, or starting from our library of customisable templates for any project, topic or target group
  • Easy participation through shareable links and QR codes
  • A live dashboard for monitoring and managing contributions
  • Built-in tools for analysing data, revealing spatial patterns and identifying action priorities
  • Exportable data, maps and reports for planning and decision-making
  • Onboarding and ongoing platform support

Spatial Reporting Interfaces

City Layers gives residents a live map to report issues, concerns, and suggestions exactly where they occur, so city teams see a real-time picture instead of scattered complaints. This is platform-led, with optional campaign support for launch and ongoing moderation.

What City Layers Delivers
  • Live issue map

    A real-time view of where public space issues are reported as they happen.

  • Maintenance prioritisation

    Clear evidence for deciding what to fix first and where.

  • Resident-to-team channel

    A direct line between what residents notice and where city teams act.

Where This Applies
Issue Reporting Neighbourhood Improvement Maintenance Prioritisation Safety Reporting Community Concern Mapping Local Action Planning
